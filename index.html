About Me

My name is Ruisheng Zhang (张瑞升 in Chinese). I am a second-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Industrial and Operations Engineering at the University of Michigan, working with Prof.Amelia C. Warden.

My research interests center around the development of advanced algorithms and methodologies aimed at solving real-world problems, enhancing user experiences, and augmenting human capabilities in human-machine/computer interactions. Broadly, my research areas include, but are not limited to:

  • Human-computer interaction research in virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR)
  • Human factors and ergonomics studies to enhance perception, cognition, and decision-making
  • Multi-modal intelligent adaptive interfaces and intention-driven implicit interactions

I received my Master’s degree in Design from the Advanced Human-Computer Interaction Lab at Southeast University, working with Prof.Xiaozhou Zhou. I also earned my Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering from Southeast University. During my time at SEU, my research focused on human-computer interaction, virtual reality, and intention recognition.
